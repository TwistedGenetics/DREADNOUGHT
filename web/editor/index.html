The FM-1, live: everything here does what it does on the FM-1, and the screen shows what the FM-1's shows. Knobs and encoders: drag up or down, or the wheel (shift: faster). Buttons: click to press; shift-click holds one down (for SAVE + FX, HOME + SELECT, SEQ + a key…), click again to let go. Click a tab to go to that page. Your computer's keys play the keys: Z S X D C… F3–E4, Q 2 W 3 E… F4–G5. The tabs above edit everything in depth.

SUB: an SH-101-style bass. The DIRT drives it after the amp; SPLIT keeps the low end clean under it. Put it on a track on the Project tab.

Each pad plays a sample (yours from the Project tab, or the built-in loops) from START to END. On the FM-1 the pads are DRUM's rows and the first 16 keys. Click a pad to pick it and hear it. Pads in the same CHOKE group cut each other.

Up to 8 zones, each a sample over a range of keys, played at its ROOT's pitch. Overlapping ranges: the first zone that covers a key plays it. LOOP goes round while the note sounds; SUST while the key is held, then plays on to the end. Click a zone's row to pick it; click the keyboard to hear a note.

ALL KEYS

Hear it here: On the FM-1 (hold):

A six-operator FM synth that plays DX7 voices: the factory's, and the ones you add from a DX7 bank on the Project tab (Add files… takes .syx). The macros go on top of the voice without changing it: BRIGHT the modulators' levels, TIME every envelope, ATTACK and RELEASE the carriers'.

Each synth has its own four LFOs (REESE's, then SUB's), and they are part of its sound. Each goes to one place (REESE's filter, pitch, level…, the breaks' pitch, decay, crush, level, or another LFO's depth). SYNC locks the rate to the tempo. Draw on a shape to make your own (DRAW): it plays as many points a cycle as you set, SMOOTH from steps to slopes. The red line: where it is now.

The FM-1 runs DREADNOUGHT 0.8 or older: update it to 0.9 for the LFOs.

Save and load

A project is everything on the FM-1 for one piece: the sounds, the tempo, every track's instrument, sends and effects, all 8 patterns of every track, and the samples with their slices, plus the original sample files added here, so you can change how they're converted later. Stop the FM-1 first. Loading sends only what differs: if it's cut off, load again and it carries on.

    Samples

    Drop WAV (or MP3, AIFF…) files here. Each goes on the FM-1 in mono, at the rate you pick: lower rates fit more (and 26 kHz is the SP-1200's). Fit to space picks the rates so they all fit, the longest samples lowered first. Listen with ▶ before you send. The breaks play them (ARP: the BRK page, PRESETS for the loop). DX7 banks (.syx) go here too: pick the voices you want and they go on with the samples, for FM (128 bytes a voice).
