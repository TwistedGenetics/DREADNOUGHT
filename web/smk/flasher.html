Before you start

Installing DREADNOUGHT replaces M-VAVE's firmware and erases what it stored: its projects, DX7 voices and samples. Back them up first with M-VAVE's workbench. Use desktop Chrome or Edge, a USB data cable, and close any other app that uses MIDI (DAWs, other update pages). Keep the keyboard powered and plugged in until the page says it is done.

DREADNOUGHT is not M-VAVE's software and is not supported by M-VAVE. To go back, use Restore M-VAVE V16 below.
