# DREADNOUGHT host scenario: chop mode (0.8). The SLICE page: start empty, a cursor (K2 coarse, K3 fine, K4 zoom),
# REC chops at the cursor (or where the loop plays, PLAY on this page), FX moves the selected chop to the cursor,
# HOME deletes it (held 1 s: all). The keys pick a chop. Chops keep the pattern's audio and save themselves.
# --- pattern steps on BREAKS A first (DRUM page): slices 2, 3, 5 on steps 1, 3, 5

expect trk 2

turn K1 1
tapkey 4 60
turn K1 2
tapkey 7 60

expect nt.2.3 2
expect nt.2.5 4

tap ARP
expect page 10
turn K1 1
expect slsel 1
turn K4 3
turn K2 1
expect cursor >5000
expect cursor <7000

expect smode.1 2
expect slsel 2

expect sl.1.3 <7000
expect nt.2.1 1
expect nt.2.3 3
expect nt.2.5 5
shot chop-01-inserted.png

expect nt.2.3 2
expect nt.2.5 4
