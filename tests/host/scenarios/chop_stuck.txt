# DREADNOUGHT host scenario (0.9): chopping by key. "Chop knob won't move off chop 1": with one chop KNOB 1 moves the
# cursor; with REC lit each key puts its own chop (key 1 = chop 1, key 2 = chop 2 ...), in order, never silently.
expect trk 1
tap ARP
tap ARP
expect page 9
turn K2 2
expect smode.1 2
expect ns.1 1
tap ARP
expect page 10
expect cursor 0
shot stuck-01-empty.png
# one chop: KNOB 1 moves the cursor (two eighths of the loop)
turn K1 2
expect cursor >19000
expect cursor <20000
# REC: keys cut. Key 2 (index 1) adds chop 2 at the cursor
tap REC
tapkey 1 5
expect ns.1 2
expect sl.1.2 >19000
expect sl.1.2 <20000
shot stuck-02-key.png
# key 4 before key 3: refused, it says why
turn K2 2
tapkey 3 5
expect ns.1 2
shot stuck-03-refused.png
tapkey 2 5
expect ns.1 3
expect sl.1.3 >38000
# out of order: the cursor back before chop 2, key 3: refused (chop 3 must come after 2)
turn K2 -2
tapkey 2 5
expect sl.1.3 >38000
# a retake: chop 2 again, later (half way to chop 3): it moves, nothing added
turn K2 1
tapkey 1 5
expect ns.1 3
expect sl.1.2 >29000
expect sl.1.2 <29500
# the pattern's steps on a chop keep that chop when it moves (no renumbering)
expect slsel 1
# by ear: the loop playing, key 4 on a hit
tap PLAY
wait 700
tapkey 3 5
expect ns.1 4
tap PLAY
# REC off: the keys play and select again
tap REC
tapkey 2 60
expect slsel 2
expect ns.1 4
